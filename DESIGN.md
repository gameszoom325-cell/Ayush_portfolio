## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an immersive, cinematic digital portfolio tailored for an emerging computer science and machine learning practitioner. Balancing the precision-engineered restraint of Apple's industrial presentation with the fluid, spatial gravitas of modern creative studios like Lusion, the identity completely eschews juvenile hacker tropes, terminal greens, or gratuitous gaming RGB in favor of institutional luxury, scientific rigor, and editorial clarity.

The visual direction centers on cinematic minimalism grounded in deep atmospheric dark tones, micro-tuned glassmorphic refraction, and physical-grade metallic accents. The interface acts as a silent gallery: spatial, deliberate, and quiet, allowing deep learning architectures, computational models, and research projects to take center stage with high-caliber weight and dignity. Micro-interactions utilize low-friction magnetism, subtle luminance shifts, and frictionless kinetic responses that feel analog and expensive.

## Colors

The palette operates on a controlled spectrum of obsidian depths, cold metallic silvers, and disciplined jade titanium accents:

- **Canvas & Surface Architecture**: The primary canvas rests on `#08080a` (Obsidian), paired with elevated surface containers of `#121216` (Deep Charcoal) and `#1a1a22` (Subtle Smoked Basalt).
- **Primary & Accent Luminescence**: `#e2e8f0` provides crisp metallic silver highlights for focal elements and display typography. Pure white (`#ffffff`) is reserved strictly for maximum focal contrast on active interactive states.
- **Secondary Atmospheric Accent**: `#2dd4bf` (Refined Jade) serves as a measured, mathematical highlight for status nodes, algorithmic performance metrics, and machine learning model indicators. It must never appear saturated or electric; apply it with restrained opacity or as soft linear accents.
- **Tertiary Neutral**: `#94a3b8` (Titanium) delivers understated secondary structural information and structural borders.
- **Sub-Surface Translucency**: Frosted boundaries and containers rely on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` overlaid with dynamic blur to simulate diamond-cut crystal and physical titanium framing.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy merges editorial stature with mathematical lucidity. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric warmth paired with razor-sharp kerning. Headings are tightly tracked (`-0.02em` to `-0.035em`) to evoke precision craftsmanship and modern Apple product keynote weight.
- **Body & Narrative (`Inter`)**: Employs neutral, utilitarian clarity with generous line-heights, ensuring research methodologies and academic achievements read with effortless authority.
- **Labels, Telemetry & Metadata (`Space Grotesk`)**: Applied to indexes, taxonomy tags, mathematical variables, and timeline markers. Rendered with extended tracking and uppercase treatment to evoke high-tech instrumentation panels and luxury mechanical dials.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architectural and cinematic, utilizing a responsive 12-column fluid grid system pinned inside a disciplined dynamic container with generous negative space:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and minimum `4rem` margins. Major editorial anchors span 8 to 10 columns to preserve intentional breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins. Complex data visualizations stack vertically into paired cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Edge padding is optimized for one-thumb reach while maintaining full-bleed visual rhythm.
- **Rhythm & Vertical Density**: Section transitions avoid cramped dividers, utilizing substantial vertical spacing (`space-xl` scaled up dynamically to `6rem`-`8rem` on hero-to-project transitions) to evoke an expansive museum gallery experience.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical physics rather than standard drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas begins at deep obsidian (`#08080a`). Secondary floating modules use semi-translucent charcoal (`rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.7)`). Elevated modals and focal drawers leverage (`rgba(26, 26, 34, 0.85)`).
- **Physical Glassmorphism**: Containers utilize multi-layered backdrop blur (minimum `20px` to `40px` saturating blurs) framed with a razor-thin top-lit border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to simulating light glancing off chamfered crystal edges).
- **Ambient Spectral Radiance**: Elevation is reinforced via tinted, low-intensity ambient glows. Rather than black shadows, cards emit an ultra-diffused jade-titanium aura (`box-shadow: 0 20px 50px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 30px -5px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.04)`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces a disciplined, Apple-inspired continuous corner radius (squircle-like perception):

- Base interactive items (buttons, chips, inputs) adopt an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius.
- Project cards, code sandboxes, and structural containers utilize a 16px (`1rem`) radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Feature showcases and full-screen preview overlays adopt a 24px (`1.5rem`) radius (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro indicators, status beacons, and pills adopt fully rounded geometric contours to contrast gracefully against the structural grid.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA**: Machined metallic pill. Gradient background running from `#f8fafc` down to `#cbd5e1`, high-contrast obsidian typography (`#08080a`), and an inner white hairline glare. Hover triggers a subtle magnetic pull and an ambient silver flare.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Obsidian surface with `1px` translucent titanium border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`), silver text (`#e2e8f0`), and a smooth glass-sheen sweep on pointer hover.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- Surface built on charcoal glass (`rgba(18, 18, 22, 0.65)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`.
- Enclosed with a dual-layer hairline stroke: top border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, bottom border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- Cards feature subtle 3D tilt responsiveness coupled with interactive spotlighting: a soft radial beam tracks cursor movement beneath the card surface.

### Chips & Badges
- Constructed with `Space Grotesk` uppercase tracking.
- Composed of micro-tinted charcoal fills with jade accent borders (`rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.2)`). A live pulsing mini-dot (`#2dd4bf`) precedes active ML research status badges.

### Input Fields & Terminal Console
- Darkened inset surface (`#050507`) with a faint recessed inner shadow.
- Borders stay subdued at `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`, intensifying smoothly to a refined jade glow (`#2dd4bf` at 40% opacity) upon keyboard focus.
- Monospaced typography for parameters, hyperparameters, and command queries.

### Selection Controls (Radios & Toggles)
- Smooth mechanical slider switches framed in dark titanium housing with a brushed silver thumb.
- Checkboxes feature a custom minimal diamond or chamfered checkmark in pure jade, departing entirely from stock browser elements.

### Specialized AI/ML Metric Display
- Telemetry modules featuring real-time training loss curves, latency monitors, and inference rate counters designed with monochrome line-graphs and jade indicator highlights.
